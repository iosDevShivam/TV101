TV101 · Smart features

Small touches that make the TV feel yours

Each one works on its own, costs almost nothing, and is switched by one line in one settings file.

QR code

Scan it with any phone camera to open the phone remote. On Home and on the screensaver.
Holds http://<TV address>:8080. Built once, rebuilt only if the router gives the TV a new address.
Setting"phone_remote": true

Music

The song, artist, cover and progress appear on the screensaver while music plays, and fade 30 s after it stops.
Reads the media session any music app publishes (Spotify, phone over Bluetooth). YouTube Music on TV can't: it stops when covered.
Setupinstall.shgrants it once

Calendar

This month on the screensaver, today in coral. Your next event under "Up next" on Home.
Reads Google Calendar's private iCal link every 15 min: repeats, time zones, moved and cancelled events handled.
Setting"calendar_ics_url": "…"

Colour that adapts to each app

The background glows in the colour of the app you're on: red behind YouTube and Netflix, magenta behind JioCinema, blue behind Prime Video. It slides and fades as you move.
The colour is taken once from each app's own icon (its saturated pixels at 16 × 16), then reused. One soft gradient, no extra screens.
Setting"ambient_glow": truefor Home · always on in All apps

Easy to configure

Everything personal lives in one small file. Edit it, send it, done: no rebuild.
// theme/my-tv/config.json
{
  "greeting_name": "Alex",
  "weather_city": "",  // empty = auto
  "calendar_ics_url": "https://…",
  "dock_apps": ["…youtube.tv", …],
  "ambient_glow": true,
  "clear_ram_button": true
}
$ scripts/push-theme.sh theme/my-tv
Or ask Claude: "Change the weather city to London."
